# People and work

Mode: Operate. Local extension of Office and Mail; inherit DESIGN.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show who is involved and what needs a response, with the original correspondence one action away.

OWN-WORLD: Pulse semantic theme, sans typography, compact shared buttons, divided rows, Lucide icons.

STORY: Select a participant, review identity, see outstanding work first, inspect evidence, confirm or dismiss suggestions, complete and reopen work.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact People disclosure sits below message metadata. Expanded content has participant buttons, outstanding work, then collapsed past work and dismissed suggestions. Office reuses the same panel per account.

FORM: Existing-surface extension, no concept seed required. Inline disclosure preserves reading space on phones. No decorative motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
