# Meetings

Record a meeting in the Windows desktop app and get a transcript and summary you can
read from any device connected to the same environment.

## Record a meeting

Open **Meetings** from the sidebar or the command palette and choose **Record
meeting**. Transcription runs with Parakeet on your computer, and each finished
sentence is saved to the environment as you talk. Choose **Stop** when the meeting
ends.

By default the recording includes what other participants say through your
speakers or headset. To record only your microphone, turn off **Include other
participants' audio when recording meetings** in **Settings → Integrations → Voice
dictation**. The microphone is the one chosen there.

Recording is only available in the Windows desktop app. Reloading the window keeps
the recording going, but a few seconds of transcript around the reload can be
missing. If saving fails, choose **Retry save**; **Discard** gives up on that
recording.

## Summaries

When a recording stops, the environment writes a summary with your **Text
generation** model and names the meeting from it. If the summary fails, open the
meeting and try again.

## Read meetings elsewhere

Web, remote browsers and the mobile app (**Settings → Meetings**) list the
environment's meetings with their transcripts and summaries, and update live while
a meeting records. Rename or delete a meeting from any of them. **Send to thread**
starts a thread in the project you pick with the summary and transcript in the
composer, ready to review before sending.
