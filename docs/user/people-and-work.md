# People and work

With Mail alpha enabled, open a message and expand **People and work**. Pulse finds participant addresses in messages you open. Confirm the person or team behind an address before recording work with them. Correct identity lets you change the name; dismissed identities can be restored.

Choose **Add task or feedback** to record a task involving that person or a request waiting for their feedback. Give it a title and, optionally, a due date. Saving does not send an email or assign work to another user.

Outstanding work appears first. Complete a task or mark feedback resolved to move it into **Past work**. Reopen it if more work is needed. Edit changes its title, type or due date. Dismiss hides unwanted work, and Restore brings it back.

Expand an item's source to read the supporting correspondence. Recent correspondence contains messages opened in Pulse, not a complete mailbox history. A reply never automatically resolves a request. If the source is unavailable, the recorded work remains editable.

Office shows the same people and work grouped by mail account. Mail on mobile includes participant confirmation, work editing and source-message navigation. Your server must support People and work; older servers hide the panel.

This alpha keeps history within its owning environment and account. It currently supports manual tasks and feedback. Automatic Luna discovery, CRM import, identity aliases and merges, calendar connections, and integration with a universal Tasks space are not available in this build.
