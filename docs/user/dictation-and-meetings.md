# Dictation and meeting recording

Open Settings > Dictation to enable dictation on this device. Download Parakeet when prompted. The enabled preference stays on while the model downloads and prepares, but dictation is unavailable until it is ready. Cancelling or retrying the download keeps your preference.

On the Windows desktop app, hold Ctrl+Shift+Space to dictate, release to finish, and press Escape to cancel. Text is pasted into the original input. Dictation history in Settings keeps the transcript if delivery fails. Turning dictation off unregisters the shortcut without stopping an active meeting recording.

In Office > Meetings, choose Turn on for this meeting or Turn on for every meeting. Choose Microphone, Computer audio, or both, then start recording. The every-meeting preference remembers your choice; it never starts recording merely because you open Office. Change it back to Ask each time in Dictation settings.

If Parakeet is unavailable, choose Record now, transcribe when ready. Stop and save finishes the audio recording. Its transcription waits for the model and runs automatically after download and preparation, even if you leave Settings. Queued recordings survive an app restart. If Parakeet finishes downloading during a recording, preparation waits until capture stops.

Keep audio only removes a waiting recording from the transcription queue without deleting the audio. Retry transcription retries failed work. Open audio plays the saved file in your default application. Delete recording removes its audio and transcript after confirmation.

Recordings, transcripts, model files, and dictation preferences belong to the Windows computer running the desktop app. Connecting to a remote coding environment does not move audio capture to that server. Browser and native mobile clients do not capture system audio in this preview; mobile continues to use keyboard dictation.

Meeting recording and transcription do not require a meeting-notes model. This preview produces the speech transcript. A separate meeting-notes generation workflow, live draft transcripts, and speaker identification are not included yet.
