Is Pulse Meet ready to use? Not yet.

  • Where it is: the code is on qb/feat-pulse-voice with its tests passing, but it is not merged and not in any installer. Your installed 0.0.44-pulse.1 has none of it.
  • Not tried for real: no one has run it on Windows yet (T12): the hotkey, pasting, a second monitor, reloading mid-meeting.
  • Can't ship yet: the voice engine branch pulse/headless has to be pushed before an installer can be built.
  • Meetily: only Meetily's Rust audio and transcription engine came across, as a background process. Meetily's own screens did not. The screens below are Pulse Code screens built on the app's existing components.
  • Not built: choosing models and dictation history were never in scope, so they are drawn here as proposals.

Recording a meeting: every header state

Recording captures your microphone, plus other participants' audio from your speakers unless you turn that off. Each finished sentence is saved to the environment as you talk. You can move to other pages while it records. Stopping saves the rest of the transcript, then the environment writes a summary with your Text generation model and names the meeting from it.

Meeting detail

Delete applies to every device. Regenerate writes the summary again. While a meeting records, the transcript updates live here and on other devices.

Dictate into any app: the voice pill

Hold the shortcut with any app in front. The pill shows at the bottom of whichever monitor that app is on and never takes keyboard focus away from it. When you let go, the text is pasted into the app and your clipboard is put back the way it was. Use the buttons below the screen to switch the pill between its three states.

Proposed

Choose models

This would be a new Models block in Voice dictation, with one choice for dictation and one for meetings, because the two need different things. Dictation needs speed and text that appears as you speak. Meetings need accuracy on long recordings and who said what. Any model other than the bundled one downloads when you pick it. The figures come from the published benchmarks I found earlier and haven't been measured on your PC yet.

Proposed

Dictation history

This would be a new Dictations page, next to Meetings in the sidebar. It lists everything you've dictated, grouped by where it went: a chat thread, or the app you dictated into. From each entry you can copy it again, insert it into the current composer, or jump back to that thread. One decision for you: should history stay on this computer only (global dictations can include anything you typed into other apps), or be saved to the environment like meetings so you can see it on your phone? Click a chat on the left to filter.
